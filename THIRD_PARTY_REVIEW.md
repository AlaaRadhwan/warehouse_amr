**What this means [inference, not legal advice].**
* The repository only links to the models, so the repository files themselves do not redistribute them.
* The building shell and 15 shelves are **NonCommercial**, and the building and the big shelves are also **NoDerivatives**. Anything this
  project *produces from* them is where the question lies: `warehouse.pgm` (a map generated by mapping the world), screenshots,
  GIFs and videos that show them, and any published map-based figure. Whether a screenshot, a LiDAR-derived occupancy map or a
  portfolio use counts as "adapted material" or "commercial use" under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 is a legal question I cannot settle. Attribution
  (BY) is required for all CC BY / BY-NC licences whichever way that question is decided.
* A portfolio shown to prospective employers is a grey area for "non-commercial"; treat it as unresolved.
* Lowest-risk options for the owner: (a) replace the NC/ND models in the public world with self-made or CC0/CC BY geometry and regenerate
  the map and figures; (b) keep them, publish attribution, and limit published visuals to what the owner is comfortable defending;
  (c) obtain written permission from the authors. Option (a) is the cleanest for a repository that others will clone and run.
